What data we process

Krabsy is designed to collect as little data as possible. There are no user accounts and no sign-in. Without a class or a tutor (see below), we collect no personal data.

In your device's browser (localStorage): So that your learning progress is kept between visits, Krabsy stores the following information locally in your browser:

  • Your answers and learning progress for each verb (correct/incorrect, number of attempts, mastery level)
  • Best times and statistics from the speed drills
  • Language preference (German/Spanish)

This data does not leave your device. We have no access to it. You can delete it at any time in your browser settings, or remove it on the statistics page using the “Reset progress” button.

Anonymous usage statistics: Each time a page is viewed, an anonymous entry is created in our server log. It contains:

  • The page viewed (path)
  • The referrer URL (where you came from, if available)
  • Browser language
  • Screen width and device type (computer, tablet, smartphone), to optimise the layout
  • Browser identifier (user agent, e.g. “Chrome on Windows”), to filter out automated access (bots)
  • Timestamp and time spent on the page: while a page is open and visible, it sends a short signal every 15 seconds so that we can see how long people practise and play
  • Game and practice progress without content: how many questions were answered on the page and how many of them were correct, as well as game events such as “level started”, “tower built” or “level won/lost” with numbers such as level, wave or coins. Which words were asked or typed in is not transmitted. We use this to improve the difficulty of the games.
  • Country, derived from your browser's time zone setting (e.g. “Europe/Berlin” → Germany) — the time zone itself is not stored, only the country

No cookies are set and your IP address is not stored. So that we can count visits, the server creates an anonymous short value (hash) from your IP address and browser identifier using a random key that is newly generated and discarded every day at midnight (UTC). This allows page views on the same day to be grouped together, but visits on different days cannot be linked to each other. We cannot identify you.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in improving the service through audience measurement only).

What we do NOT do

  • We do not set cookies
  • We do not use tracking services such as Google Analytics, Meta Pixel or similar
  • We do not share data with third parties
  • We do not try to recognise users
  • Without a class or a tutor, we do not collect any personal data – neither from children nor from adults (for classes and tutor spaces, see below)

Classes (only if a teacher creates a class)

Teachers can create a class on Krabsy. This is optional; without a class, everything stated above applies unchanged.

  • What is stored: the name of the class, a class code, the first names that pupils enter themselves when joining (where first names are the same, with the first letter of the surname), and for each answer in games and exercises: topic, word asked (e.g. bring), form, correct or incorrect, game or exercise, and time. No surnames, no email addresses, no IP addresses, no device characteristics.
  • Saved profile: so that a pupil can continue on another device, the progress Krabsy otherwise keeps only in the browser is also saved with the pupil's key: the character they built, shells and gifts, learning statistics and game progress (levels, stars). Language and sound settings are not saved.
  • Who can see it: only people who have the teacher's private link. We do not analyse class data for any other purpose and do not pass it on.
  • Access: teachers receive a secret link, pupils a key that is kept only in their browser. On the server, both are stored only as a checksum (hash).
  • Deletion: the teacher can delete individual names or the whole class at any time. Classes with no activity are automatically deleted after 6 months, together with all names and answers. Anyone who leaves a class sends nothing further from then on.
  • Storage location: on our server in Germany (see “Server and hosting”).
  • Legal basis: the teacher or school decides on its use in lessons; we process the class data only so that the teacher can see their class's learning progress. Schools that need a data processing agreement for this can write to us at hallo@krabsy.com.

Tutor spaces (only if a tutor uses Krabsy with their students)

Private tutors can create a tutor space on Krabsy and add their students. This is optional; without it, everything stated above applies unchanged.

  • What is stored: the name the tutor gives the space and the tutor's email address, which we use only to send sign-in links (there are no passwords); and for each student: the first name the tutor types, and what the tutor writes about the student (level, goals, notes, notes on lessons and topics, star ratings, homework, and whether the student is an adult). When the student uses their personal link: each answer in games and exercises (topic, word asked, form, correct or incorrect, game or exercise, time) and the saved profile described under “Classes” (character, shells and gifts, learning statistics, game progress). No surnames, no email addresses, no IP addresses, no device characteristics.
  • Who can see it: only people who have the tutor's private link. The student's personal link shows the student their homework. If the tutor creates a report link for the parents, it shows the first name, the star ratings, the number of answers per week, homework progress, the number of lessons and a note the tutor writes for the parents; it never shows the tutor's other notes. We do not analyse this data for any other purpose and do not pass it on.
  • Access: the tutor, the student and the parents' report each have a secret link. On the server, the keys in these links are stored only as a checksum (hash). If several people use the same device (for example siblings), the device keeps their first names and keys locally so that they can choose who is playing; their progress stays separate.
  • Deletion: the tutor can remove a student or delete the whole space at any time; a new link for a student or the parents makes the old one stop working. A space that is not opened for 6 months is deleted automatically, together with all students, notes and answers.
  • Storage location: on our server in Germany (see “Server and hosting”).
  • Responsibility: the tutor decides to use Krabsy with their students and informs them before sending a link — for children, their parents, whose consent the tutor obtains. We process the data only so that the tutor can plan lessons and see their students' progress. Tutors who need a data processing agreement can write to us at hallo@krabsy.com.

Quiz Room (live quiz in class)

In the Quiz Room, a teacher opens a room and the children join with a room code. So that everyone can play together, the room runs through our server.

  • What is processed: the first name the child enters when joining, the chosen character and colour, the character's position in the room, the answers given and the points. No surnames, no email addresses, no IP addresses.
  • In memory only: this data is held only in the server's working memory while the room is open. None of it is written to disk or to a database. The room closes at the latest 2 hours after it was opened, or 15 minutes after the teacher has left it; then everything is gone.
  • Who can see it: the teacher and the other children in the room see first names, characters and the top 5. Nobody except the child can see which answer a child gave; the teacher sees how many children chose each answer and, at the end, the ranking with points and the number of correct answers per child.
  • On the child's device: as in all games, the answers count towards the learning statistics in the browser. If the child has joined a class, they are sent to the class as with the other games (see “Classes”).

Advertising

Krabsy currently shows no advertising. Should this change, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly, and a cookie banner for advertising cookies will be introduced before any advertising is shown.

Children

Krabsy is aimed at learners from around 10 years of age. Without a class or a tutor, we collect no personal data whatsoever; special consent from parents or guardians is therefore not required. Classes are set up by teachers as part of their lessons; children enter only their first name. Tutor spaces are set up by private tutors, who ask the parents before they send a child a link. Parents and teachers can let children use Krabsy without concern.

Your rights

Under the GDPR and the LOPDGDD, you have the right to:

  • Access to the data stored about you (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Object to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)

Without a class or a tutor, Krabsy stores no personal data about you, so most of these rights have no practical effect. Data in a class or a tutor space can be deleted by the teacher or tutor at any time; you can also write to us at hallo@krabsy.com.

Right to lodge a complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. The competent authorities are:

  • In Spain: Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD), https://www.aepd.es
  • In Germany: the data protection authority of your federal state, or the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (BfDI), https://www.bfdi.bund.de

Server and hosting

Krabsy is hosted on a server in Germany. Server logs are kept for a maximum of 30 days and then automatically deleted.
