How to earn shells 🐚

  • Every right answer earns 1 shell, in all games and exercises.
  • Words that are new to you, or that you are still practising, earn 1 extra shell.
  • A wrong answer costs nothing.
  • After 200 shells in one day, they come more slowly.

What you can unlock

12 figures are free from the start, and so are all colours. Other figures cost 150 to 400 shells; hats, glasses and backpacks cost 40 to 150. What you unlock stays yours.

The 3D games will get your figure one by one.

Only on this device

Your figure and your shells are saved in this browser on this device, with no account. If you clear the browser data or use another device, they are gone.
