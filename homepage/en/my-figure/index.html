My figure

Build your own figure. Practise and you get gifts 🎁: new figures and things to wear.

How to earn shells 🐚

  • Every right answer earns 1 shell, in all games and exercises.
  • Words that are new to you, or that you are still practising, earn 1 extra shell.
  • A wrong answer costs nothing.
  • After 200 shells in one day, they come more slowly.

To the games →

How to get gifts 🎁

  • Every 150 shells bring a surprise: a new figure or something to wear.
  • There are gifts for practising on 3, 7, 14 and 30 days (a day counts from 10 answers), and for every new week in which you practise again.
  • And for what you achieve: for example 50 right answers, 10 words you know well, or a verb diploma.
  • There are gifts in the 3D games too: in Verb Dungeon one waits in the chest after each boss.

You have 12 figures and all colours from the start. What you get stays yours.

Only on this device

Your figure and your shells are saved in this browser on this device, with no account. If you clear the browser data or use another device, they are gone.
