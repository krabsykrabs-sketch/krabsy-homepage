Verb Towers in Spanish

A tower-defence game. Enemies march along the road to your castle; your towers stop them. Towers cost coins, and you earn coins in the forge: answer the Spanish sentence, and every right answer forges coins. A streak of right answers pays more.

How to play

  1. Tap a glowing field next to the road and build a tower.
  2. Start the wave. While the enemies walk, answer the sentences in the forge (tap, or keys Q, W, E).
  3. A wrong answer locks the forge for a moment and shows you the right word and why.
  4. Use your coins for more towers and upgrades. Protect the castle to win stars.

Good to know

  • Works on computers, Chromebooks and tablets; on a phone, turn it sideways.
  • Stars and unlocked levels are saved on this device only. No account.
