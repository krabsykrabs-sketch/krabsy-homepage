Verb Towers: a Spanish tower-defence game

Topic: Yo soy … (ser) · Estoy … (estar) · Ser or estar? · Tengo … (tener)

Enemies march along the road to your castle, and your towers stop them. Towers cost coins, and you earn coins in the forge: answer the Spanish sentence, and every right answer forges coins. A streak of right answers pays more.

The game is free in the browser, with English menus and Spanish sentences.

How to play Verb Towers

  • Tap or click a glowing field next to the road to build a tower.
  • Start the wave. While the enemies walk, answer the sentences in the forge: tap an answer or press Q W E.
  • A wrong answer locks the forge for a moment and shows you the right word and a short rule.
  • Spend your coins on more towers and upgrades. Protect the castle to win stars and unlock the next level.

What you practice

The forge asks Spanish sentences with a gap, like Me ___ Sofía. Right answers give coins, so practicing is how you defend the castle. After a mistake you see the right word and why. There are six levels, from the Meadow to the Winter Pass.

Questions and answers

Does it work on a phone?

Yes, by tapping; turn the phone sideways. Tablets and computers show a bigger map.

What happens if I answer wrong?

The forge locks for a moment and shows the right word and a short rule. Then the next sentence comes.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, stars and unlocked levels are saved on your device only. There is no account.
