Verb Kitchen: a Spanish cooking game

Topic: Yo soy … (ser) · Estoy … (estar) · Ser or estar? · Tengo … (tener)

Welcome to Verb Kitchen! In this 3D cooking game you take orders, chop ingredients, fry and bake, and serve the dishes through the hatch. But the plates come back dirty, and they only get clean with Spanish: two right answers at the sink wash one plate.

That turns kitchen chaos into Spanish practice. The game is free in the browser, with English menus and Spanish sentences.

How to play Verb Kitchen

  • Move with W A S D or the arrow keys, pick up and put down with E, chop by holding Space.
  • On a tablet or phone, tap where your chef should go and tap a station to use it.
  • Take ingredients from the crates, chop and cook them, put them on a plate and serve. Don't let anything burn!
  • At the sink you answer Spanish sentences with one of three buttons: click them or press 1 2 3.
  • The faster you finish all orders, the more stars you get.

What you practise

Each plate asks two Spanish sentences with a gap, usually of the same kind: Yo ___ de Londres. After a mistake you see the whole sentence and a short rule in English. Sentences you missed come back a few plates later and land in the Sentence Cookbook, so the sink asks them first next time. There are eight kitchens, from a salad bistro with a tutorial to burgers, pizza, soup, ice cream and a sandwich bar.

Questions and answers

Do I need a keyboard?

On a computer you play with the keyboard. On tablets and phones tapping works: your chef walks to the place you tap.

Can I lose in Verb Kitchen?

No. Orders never run out. The clock only measures your time, and faster cooks get more stars.

What is the Sentence Cookbook?

It lists the Spanish sentences of the topic. The ones you missed are at the top, and the sink asks them first in your next round.
