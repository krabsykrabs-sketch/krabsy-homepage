Verb Kitchen in Spanish

A busy cooking game. Orders come in; you chop, cook, put the dish on a plate and serve it before the customer runs out of patience. Every served dish comes back as a dirty plate, and you wash it at the sink by answering two Spanish sentences. No clean plates, no serving!

How to play

  1. Move with WASD or the arrow keys (on a tablet: the stick), pick things up with E, chop with Space.
  2. Follow the order cards at the top: cook the dish, put it on a plate, take it to the hatch.
  3. Out of plates? Go to the sink and press E: two right answers wash one plate.
  4. A wrong answer shows the whole sentence and why. That sentence comes back later, and it's saved in the Cookbook for next time.

Good to know

  • Best on a computer or Chromebook with a keyboard; it also works on tablets.
  • Stars and unlocked levels are saved on this device only. No account.
