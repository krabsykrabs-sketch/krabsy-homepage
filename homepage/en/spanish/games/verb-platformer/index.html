Verb Platformer: a Spanish jump-and-run

Jump across floating islands high above the clouds. On the way to the flag you reach answer gates: a Spanish sentence with a gap floats above three red blocks, each with a word. Land on the right one. The wrong blocks crumble and fall!

The game is free in the browser, with English menus and Spanish sentences.

How to play Verb Platformer

  • Run with W A S D or the arrow keys, jump with Space and look around with the mouse. On a tablet: the stick and the jump button.
  • Read the sentence on the orange sign, then jump on the block with the missing word.
  • Wrong block? You fall into the clouds, see the right sentence and why, and try again from the last checkpoint.
  • Reach the flag. Fewer wrong answers mean more stars.

What you practise

Every gate is one Spanish sentence with a gap, for example Soy ___ Inglaterra. After a wrong block the game shows the whole sentence and a short rule in English. The first levels ask the easiest sentences; later levels mix in harder ones. There are six levels, from First Steps to the Sky Finale with hammers and moving platforms.

Questions and answers

Does it work on a tablet?

Yes, with a stick and a jump button. It is best on a computer or Chromebook with a big screen.

How do I get three stars?

Answer every gate right the first time. One or two wrong answers give two stars, more give one. Falling into a gap costs no stars.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, stars and best times are saved on your device only. There is no account.
