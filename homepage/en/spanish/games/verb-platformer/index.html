Verb Platformer in Spanish

A jump-and-run high above the clouds. On the way to the flag you reach answer gates: a Spanish sentence with a gap floats above three blocks, each with a word. Jump on the block with the right word. The wrong blocks crumble and fall!

How to play

  1. Run with WASD or the arrow keys, jump with Space, look around with the mouse (on a tablet: the stick and the jump button).
  2. Read the sentence on the orange sign, then jump on the block with the missing word.
  3. Wrong block? You fall, see the right sentence and why, and try again from the last checkpoint.
  4. Reach the flag. Fewer wrong answers = more stars.

Good to know

  • Best on a computer or Chromebook; it also works on tablets. Phones only in landscape and with small text.
  • Stars and best times are saved on this device only. No account.
