Egg Island: a Spanish island adventure

Topic: Yo soy … (ser) · Estoy … (estar) · Ser or estar? · Tengo … (tener)

Run across a sunny 3D island and look for eggs. To pick an egg up you answer a Spanish sentence. Carry it to your farm before the thief crabs grab it, put it in the incubator and watch a creature hatch.

Your creatures earn coins for upgrades, and rarer eggs ask more. The game is free in the browser, with English menus and Spanish sentences.

How to play Egg Island

  • Walk with W A S D, turn the camera by dragging the mouse, run with Shift and jump with Space. On a tablet: the stick and the hand button (turn the tablet sideways).
  • Walk to a glowing egg and press E (or click it). Answer the sentence to pick the egg up.
  • Follow the arrow back to your farm. The egg goes into the incubator and hatches after a moment.
  • Watch out for thief crabs: answer right and you keep your egg. Spend the coins your creatures earn on upgrades (U) and look at your creatures in the Creature Book (B).

What you practise

Each egg asks a Spanish sentence with a gap, like Me ___ Sofía. Rare eggs ask two. After a mistake the egg rolls away, and you see the whole sentence and a short rule in English. The sentence comes back soon, and the Creature Book lists the sentences you still need to practise.

Questions and answers

Does it work on a phone?

It needs a wide screen: a computer, a Chromebook or a tablet held sideways. Phones are not tested yet.

Can I lose in Egg Island?

No. A wrong answer only lets the egg roll away; it comes back soon. Thief crabs can take an egg, but you can catch them.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, your creatures, coins and upgrades are saved on your device only. There is no account.
