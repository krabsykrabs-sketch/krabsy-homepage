Live quiz

The Quiz Room: live with your class or one pupil

Every pupil walks through a 3D room as a little character and answers on their own screen. You lead the room, they play.

  1. Open a roomChoose the topic, difficulty, number of questions and time. You see every question first and can swap or remove any.
  2. Pupils joinWith the room code or QR code and their first name. No account, up to 40 pupils.
  3. Start the quizAfter each question: the answer and the top 5. At the end: the sentences the class got wrong most often.

New · For private tutors

Live 1:1 lessons without screen sharing

Keep your video call for face and voice. Your student opens their own Krabsy link, and you see on your screen what they do: every answer, the letters as they type, even their pointer. Add material during the lesson, point at things, give a hint or a star.

  1. Create your spaceWith your email — no password; we send you a sign-in link.
  2. Add your studentsFirst names only. Each student gets a personal link that stays the same.
  3. Teach liveStart the lesson, show worksheets, quick practice or a picture book, and keep a note for every lesson.
