Spanish grammar practice on Krabsy

Krabsy is a free learning site with real browser games. In the Spanish section, children practise Spanish grammar while they cook in a busy kitchen, jump across the clouds or build towers. Every game asks short Spanish sentences with a gap, and after a mistake it shows the right sentence and a short rule.

Yo soy … (ser)

The first topic is for beginners: say who you are, where you are from and what you are like. Yo soy Lucas. Soy de Inglaterra. Soy alto. More topics are on the way, chosen together with Spanish teachers.

For whom?

For children learning Spanish at school or in lessons, and for their teachers. Everything works without an account on computers, tablets and many phones; progress is saved only on your device.
