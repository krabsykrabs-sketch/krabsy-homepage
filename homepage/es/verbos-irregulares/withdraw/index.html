withdraw – withdrew – withdrawn

Inglés withdraw = retirar, retirarse

Forma basePasado simpleParticipio
withdrawwithdrewwithdrawn
3.ª persona (he/she/it): withdrawsGerundio (-ing): withdrawing

Frases de ejemplo con traducción

  • Pasado simpleHe withdrew fifty euros from the cash machine.Sacó cincuenta euros del cajero.
  • ParticipioShe has withdrawn from the competition.Se ha retirado de la competición.
  • Pregunta (pasado simple)How much money did you withdraw for the trip?¿Cuánto dinero sacaste para el viaje?
  • Negación (pasado simple)Mum didn't withdraw any money at the airport.Mamá no sacó dinero en el aeropuerto.
  • Presente (he/she/it)The snail withdraws into its shell when you touch it.El caracol se esconde en su concha cuando lo tocas.

Errores típicos

✗ She has withdrew from the race.

✓ She has withdrawn from the race.

Después de have/has/had va el participio withdrawn, no el pasado withdrew.

✗ I withdrawed ten pounds from the bank.

✓ I withdrew ten pounds from the bank.

withdraw es irregular: «withdrawed» no existe. Lo correcto es withdrew (pasado) y withdrawn (participio).

Preguntas frecuentes

¿Cuál es el pasado de withdraw?
El pasado (pasado simple) de withdraw es withdrew; el participio es withdrawn.
¿Cuál es el participio de withdraw?
El participio (past participle) de withdraw es withdrawn. Se usa después de have/has/had, por ejemplo en el present perfect: have withdrawn.
¿Cómo se dice withdraw en pasado?
En pasado, withdraw se dice withdrew; con have/has se usa el participio withdrawn. Ejemplo: «He withdrew fifty euros from the cash machine.» = «Sacó cincuenta euros del cajero.» En preguntas y negaciones se usa did + forma base: did … withdraw?, didn't withdraw.
¿Cuál es el gerundio (forma -ing) de withdraw?
withdrawing: basta con añadir -ing (withdraw → withdrawing). En presente, con he/she/it se usa withdraws.
