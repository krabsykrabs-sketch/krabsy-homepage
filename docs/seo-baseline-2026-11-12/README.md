# SEO baseline — 2026-11-12 (before the SEO overhaul deploy)

Raw exports for the December comparison. Headline numbers:

- Google: 83 indexed, 314 not indexed (286 "Gecrawlt – zurzeit nicht indexiert"),
  36 impressions / 11 clicks in 3 months (brand queries only).
- Of the 286 crawled-not-indexed: 257 are verb pages (121 DE + 136 ES), i.e.
  ~75 % of all verb pages. Rejection rate by tier: tier 1 73/86, tier 2 95/126,
  tier 3 89/132 — Google rejected common verbs at least as often as rare ones.
- Game pages, hubs, homepages, legal pages were NOT in the list.
- Bing: 30,100 impressions / 42 clicks (Aug 12 – Nov 10). Copilot citations
  ~120/day in Nov.
- Changes deployed the same day: see WORK_LOG 2026-11-12 (incl. Google-only
  noindex on the 132 tier-3 verb pages).
