  posts per day. Materials are the living part; the forums are not.

## Precedent

englishonline.training ("ohne Anmeldung, ohne Werbung", Klasse 7–10) added 7
links under its own name between 23.09 and 24.11.2026, including deep topic
pages, all live. Older materials with links to the author's own site or
videos are tolerated. Makers submitting their own links is accepted.

## Positioning

Not "another site" but **the modern companion to the paper worksheets**:
practise on paper, check yourself online, then the Verb-Diplom. Free, no
login, no ads, answer keys included, current (2026), works on tablets and
phones, and a class code that shows the teacher which verbs to repeat.
Tone: a fellow contributor, not an advertiser. One footnote, no logos all
over the sheet.
