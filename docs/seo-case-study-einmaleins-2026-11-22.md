# Case study: einmaleins.de network (2026-11-22)

Research only, from public pages. No browser and no backlink tool (Ahrefs free checker did
not work through fetch), so there are no backlink counts; rankings are not verified.

## What it is

- einmaleins.de: free times-table practice for Klasse 2–4, by BVK Productions B.V. (Utrecht,
  two brothers, since 2005). Funded by **Google AdSense** (4–12 ad units per page), no
  premium tier. Free teacher/parent accounts with classes and progress.
- **23 domains**, one per market, mostly exact-match keyword domains, several of them
  bought after being parked: timestables.com (parked until ~2016), einmaleins.de (parked
  until ~2018, moved from einmaleinslernen.de with a 301), tafeldiploma.nl (the original, 2004–06).
  Country splits even within one language (DE/AT/CH, NL/BE, US/UK/NZ).
- One template and CDN for all; games are iframed from tafeldiploma.nl. Every page links all
  23 domains in the footer and in reciprocal hreflang.
- Localised per school system: NL "groep 4/5", DE "Klasse 2 und 3", UK "Multiplication
  Tables Check"; local-language URL slugs; seasonal pages (Weihnachten, Halloween).

## On-page

- Small: **81 URLs** in the einmaleins.de sitemap: 12 table pages, 12 worksheet pages,
  ~35 game pages, tests/diploma/hubs.
- One keyword per page, exact in title and H1 ("7er-Einmaleins üben",
  "Kostenlose 7er-Reihe-Arbeitsblätter"). Table pages ~1,000 words with the whole exercise
  on the page; every table has a worksheet twin page with PDFs.
- Game pages carry a templated teacher block ("Leitfaden für den Unterricht",
  learning goals, "kompatibel mit digitalen Klassenzimmern").
- No schema markup, no FAQ markup.

## Where the links come from (verified examples)

- **School websites' "useful links" pages dominate**: richard-grundschule.de ("Digitales
  Lernen", links the 100-second test specifically), vs-bruck.tsn.at (links einmaleins.at),
  littlebourne.kent.sch.uk (timestables.co.uk); many more UK school pages in search results
  (not individually verified).
- Small link lists (mathe-lernen.net); 2020 homeschooling lists (now partly 404).
- **Not found:** education portals (Bildungsserver, Elixier, ZUM, WirLernenOnline,
  4teachers, Lehrer-Online), Wikipedia, press.

## Lessons for Krabsy

1. **School link pages are the durable link source.** Target English-department / "Links"
   pages of Gymnasien and Realschulen (DE/AT), colegios' "recursos" pages (ES/LatAm).
2. **A named feature teachers can link** (they link "the 100-second test"). Candidate:
   a "Verb-Diplom" / "100 Verben in 3 Minuten" on its own URL.
3. **Printable worksheet twin pages** per topic/group; schools link worksheets directly.
4. **Teacher notes on each game/drill page** ("so nutzen Sie es im Unterricht").
5. **Few, dense pages, one intent each.** ~80 URLs is enough; supports the tier-3 noindex
   direction.
6. **Localise to school systems** (Klasse 5–7 / Lehrplan; ESO / primaria cursos), seasonal pages.
7. **Don't copy the domain strategy.** Their domains were bought exact-match names;
   keep krabsy.com with language folders. Country variants later as hreflang folders.
8. Ads: they show AdSense on a kids' site; Krabsy's "no ads" stays a selling point.
