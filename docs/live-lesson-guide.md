# Live lesson on Krabsy — setup guide (for tutors and students)

Zoom stays for face and voice. **Nobody shares a screen.** The student works on
Krabsy, the tutor sees it live.

## Once: the tutor's space (tutor)

1. Open **krabsy.com/en/spanish/tutors/** → "No tutor space yet? Create one" → your
   name and email → **Create my space**.
2. Click the link in the email ("Create your Krabsy tutor space"). You are in your
   space. (Check the spam folder if it doesn't arrive.)
3. **+ Add a student**: first name, level, tick "Adult learner" for adults.
4. On the student's page: **Personal link → Copy** and send it to the student (or the
   parents). The student saves it as a bookmark. It is the same link for every lesson.

## Every lesson

**Student**
1. Join the Zoom call as usual.
2. Open your **Krabsy link** (the bookmark).
3. **Minimize Zoom**: Windows: the **–** at the top right of the Zoom window; Mac: the
   yellow button. Zoom keeps a **small video window** with the tutor's face on top of
   everything. (If it disappears: click the Zoom icon in the taskbar / dock.)
4. Leave the Krabsy page open. When the lesson starts, it switches by itself.

**Tutor**
1. In your space, click **Start live lesson** on the student's card.
2. Wait for the green dot **"… is here"** (if not: ask the student to open their link).
3. **+ Add** → pick a Krabsy material, or **Quick practice** (topic, 6 or 10 questions,
   type / choose / drag), or a saved worksheet → **Show now**.
4. You see what the student sees: answers as they come, letters as they type, their
   pointer. Your tools: **Point** (click on your view → it pulses on their screen),
   **Hint**, **Show answer**, **◀ ▶** (steps and book pages), **⭐**, **🎁**, **Notes**.
5. **End lesson** → summary → edit the note → **Save and end**. The note appears
   under the student's lesson notes.

Tip for the tutor: put Krabsy and Zoom **side by side** on your screen; the live page
works in a narrow window.

## If something goes wrong

- The student's page doesn't switch: the student **reloads** the Krabsy page.
- "… is not here": the student opens the link again (it must be their own link).
- Something looks stuck on either side: **reload**. The lesson continues where it was.
- During a Krabsy update the lesson can drop for a moment: both pages reconnect; show
  the material again with + Add.
